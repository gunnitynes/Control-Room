1What it is

gunnitynes.github.io/Control-Room

Control Room runs on your computer and the projector.

First of allJoining is checking in.

When a student joins with their phone and picks their name, their attendance is marked. Control Room is an attendance tool first, with the games on top: every lesson starts with everyone checking in. With a Google Sheet the list is written for you (section 5); without one, you copy it with one click (no sheet).

Control Room opens on the Attendance screen: a big QR code in the middle for students to scan, your class list and who has checked in. If attendance is all you need, that’s the only screen you use. Activities opens the games and tools; Attendance in the top bar brings you back.

Next to the games there are everyday classroom tools in the top bar: a room vote, a timer, a random picker and a screensaver.

Students join with their phones, and the phones become buzzers, faders, knobs, voting cards and remix pads. Twenty-three modules: quizzes, ear training and creative sprints.

Nothing to install, no accounts. It is a website: open it in Google Chrome on the computer connected to the projector and speakers. Students only need a phone with a camera and a browser. Phones and your computer need internet (they find each other through a free relay, then talk directly).

What you need Your computer with Chrome, on the projector and the room speakers · students’ phones · internet in the room.
Optional: students’ own computers with a DAW (Ableton Live, Logic …) for the modules that send sound and files (section 4).

2Setup

once per computer · about two minutes

  1. Open Control Room

    In Chrome, go to gunnitynes.github.io/Control-Room.

  2. Tell it who’s teaching

    The first time, it asks “Who’s teaching?”. Type your name, e.g. Gunni. Student computers find your class by this name, so several teachers can run Control Room at the same time in different rooms. If a colleague has the same first name, use your last name too. You can change it any time under Teacher on the main menu.

  3. Connect your class list (optional)

    With your own Google Sheet, students tap their name instead of typing it, and every check-in lands on the sheet as attendance. Set it up once, see section 5.

  4. Copy your teacher link

    Under Teacher on the main menu: ⧉ Copy teacher link. It is your Control Room: open it on any computer and it starts set up as you, with your name and, once connected, your Google Sheet. Save it as a bookmark or in an email to yourself, If you connect your sheet later, copy it again.

One browser, one computerYour class list link, your music folders and your settings are saved in this Chrome on this computer. Teaching from another computer? Use your teacher link: one click and that computer is set up as you.

3Two ways to run it

pick per lesson, mix freely

Either way students join with one scan, and either way picking their name checks them in on your attendance sheet.

Way A · phones only

Control Room as is

Students scan the QR code on the big screen with their phone camera and tap their name. That’s it.

  • Every quiz, ear-training game and most creative modules work like this.
  • All sound comes from your computer and the room speakers.
  • Press Q any time to show the join code big. Latecomers can join mid-game.
  • Without a class list, students type their first name instead, and you copy the attendance list from the main menu (no sheet).
Way B · phone + computer

With CADCAST

Each student opens the CADCAST page on their own computer. It shows a QR code; they scan that with their phone and tap their name.

  • One scan signs them in twice: the phone joins Control Room, and the computer becomes theirs.
  • Their computer can now send you its sound (what their DAW plays) and send and receive files: missions, materials, recordings, finished parts.
  • Needed for Supply Drop, The Conductor and The Class Album; it adds files to Constraint Generator and the relay to Soundabout Roundabout.
You don’t have to choose for the whole lessonA student who joined with the big-screen code can pair their computer later: they open the CADCAST page and scan it with the same phone.

4Student computers

CADCAST: sound and files between their computer and yours

Every lesson

  1. Students open the CADCAST page

    On every student computer, gunnitynes.github.io/CADCAST/go/ is already bookmarked. It finds your class by itself. Only if it doesn’t, it asks for “Your teacher’s name”: students type your name (the same as in Control Room) once.

  2. Open the Mixer to hear them

    Click Mixer at the top right of Control Room. It opens the CADCAST patch bay in a new tab of the same Chrome (allow pop-ups if Chrome asks). Every paired computer appears as a strip with its student’s name and a fader. The Mixer must stay open in that browser for The Conductor.

On each student computer, once

CADCAST hears exactly what the student’s DAW plays. The school computers are ready for it: BlackHole (a virtual audio cable) is installed, and an output device called CADCAST sends the sound to the student’s audio interface and to BlackHole at the same time. So the student still hears themselves, and CADCAST hears it too.

  1. The CADCAST output device

    This step may already have been done by a technician. Check in Audio MIDI Setup that a device called CADCAST exists. If not:

    1. Open Audio MIDI Setup (Spotlight: ⌘ Space, type “Audio MIDI”).
    2. Bottom left + ▸ Create Multi-Output Device. Double-click its name and call it CADCAST.
    3. Tick the audio interface (or headphones) and BlackHole 2ch. Tick Drift Correction on BlackHole.
  2. Select CADCAST as the audio output

    In the DAW, choose CADCAST as the audio output device. Ableton Live: Settings ▸ Audio ▸ Audio Output Device. Logic: Settings ▸ Audio ▸ Output Device.

  3. Open the CADCAST page and pair

    Open the bookmark in Chrome. It shows a QR code: scan it with the phone, tap your name. The phone says “Your computer is now …”. Chrome asks to use the microphone: Allow. It only reads BlackHole, never the real microphone. Then press Connect.

What travels through CADCAST To you: their sound (live, in the Mixer) · Roundabout recordings · their finished part for The Class Album.
To them: you can send files to every student computer, such as audio, MIDI or project zips, through several Control Room modules. Files appear on their CADCAST page, and they drag them straight into the DAW.

A computer stays paired for 12 hours, so reloading the page reconnects on its own. Not you? on the page shows the code again for a different student.

5Attendance sheet

your own Google Sheet · set up once, about five minutes

Students tap their name on the phone and they are checked in on your Google Sheet, with the date and time. You build the sheet, paste a small script into it and give Control Room its link.

No Google Sheet? Copy the list Students type their first name when they join. On the main menu, just under the Teacher box, click the yellow ⧉ Copy attendance button: it copies everyone who joined this room, sorted by name, with the time they joined, under a heading with the date. Paste it into an email, a document or a spreadsheet (each name and time lands in its own column).
Copy it before you click New room: a new room starts an empty list. The list also covers anyone who joined and left, unless you removed them by clicking their name. It works with a Google Sheet too, as a quick check.

  1. Go to sheets.new while signed in to Google. Give the file a name, e.g. Attendance 2026.
  2. One tab per class. The tab’s name is the class name students see (double-click the tab to rename it). Add a tab with + bottom left.
  3. In each tab: a heading in A1 (anything, e.g. “Student name”). The names start in row 2: one student per row from A2 down. Anything in row 1 is treated as a heading and never shown. Write names the way students should see them.
  4. You don’t have to make the MasterLog tab: the script adds it.

Step 2 · Add the script

  1. Copy the script from Control Room

    Main menu ▸ Class list ▸ ＋ Connect your own Google Sheet…. That card has a Copy the script button: it copies the whole script, with the setup instructions written at the top of it. The card shows these same steps too.

  2. Paste it into your sheet

    In your sheet: Extensions ▸ Apps Script. Select everything that is there (⌘A / Ctrl A), then paste (⌘V / Ctrl V).

  3. Save

    The disk icon or ⌘S. If it asks for a project name, type Control Room.

Step 3 · Deploy it as a web app

This turns the script into a private link that only Control Room uses.

  1. New deployment

    Top right of the editor: the blue Deploy button ▸ New deployment. Next to “Select type”, click the gear ⚙ ▸ Web app.

  2. The two settings that matter

    Description: anything (Control Room). Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Not “Anyone with a Google account”: phones aren’t signed in to Google, so that would block them.

  3. Authorise it

    Click Deploy ▸ Authorize access and pick your account. If Google says “Google hasn’t verified this app”: Advanced ▸ Go to Control Room (unsafe) ▸ Allow. It is your own script, and it can only read and write this one sheet.

  4. Copy the Web app URL

    The long link ending in /exec, with its Copy button. Test it: paste it into a new browser tab. You should see your classes, like {"ok":true,"classes":["PROD-1A","PROD-1B"]}.

Step 4 · Link it to Control Room

  1. Paste the link

    Main menu ▸ Class list ▸ ＋ Connect your own Google Sheet…. Step 4 of that card: paste the /exec link and press Connect. Control Room checks it and shows how many classes it found.

  2. Each lesson: pick the class

    Main menu ▸ Class list ▸ choose the class. Phones now show that class’s names to tap. Below the menu you see how many are checked in today; names on the room panel get a mark when the sheet has them.

Your sheet link: keep it in your files

The /exec link is how Control Room reaches your sheet. It never changes, and it is the only thing you need to connect the sheet on another computer.

  1. Copy it any time

    Main menu ▸ Class list ▸ ⧉ Copy your sheet link. (Also in the sheet card, next to Disconnect.) Save it somewhere, like in a note or an email to yourself.

  2. On another computer

    Easiest: open your teacher link, which brings your name and sheet with it. Or if you don’t have your teacher link handy, open Control Room, type your name, then Class list ▸ ＋ Connect your own Google Sheet… and paste the sheet link in step 4. Either way: no script and no deploying again.

Something not working?
  • “Can’t reach the attendance sheet”: in step 3, Who has access must be Anyone. Check that you copied the Web app URL ending in /exec, not the editor’s address.
  • No classes show up: each class needs its own tab, with the names in column A from row 2. The MasterLog tab is not a class.
  • “Anyone” isn’t offered: some school Google accounts block it. Make the sheet with a personal Google account.
  • You edited the script later: Deploy ▸ Manage deployments ▸ the pencil ✎ ▸ Version: New version ▸ Deploy. The link stays the same. A New deployment makes a new link that you would have to paste again.
  • Change or remove the link: Class list ▸ ⚙ Your Google Sheet: change link… or ✕ Disconnect this sheet.

6Running a lesson

the rhythm of a Control Room class

  1. Students check in

    Control Room opens on the Attendance screen with a big QR code: students scan it and tap their name (or use Way B, section 3). Names appear on the right as they check in. Then click Activities for the games, or stay here if you only take attendance.

  2. Pick a module, set it up

    Click a module. Its setup screen has the options (rounds, time, difficulty, categories …) and a short “how it works”. Your choices are remembered for next time. Or let the class choose with a room vote.

  3. Play

    Space moves on. Phones follow by themselves. Click the logo top left to go back to the menu.

  4. Finish

    Every end screen offers Play another round (R). Points across the lesson add up in the room standings; the leaderboard only appears when you click Show the leaderboard. No Google Sheet? Click ⧉ Copy attendance on the main menu before you close (no sheet).

Keys on your computer

SpaceNext, start, reveal
PPause the round clock (or click the ring)
AAutoplay on or off: the next round starts by itself
RPlay another round with the same settings
QShow the join code big
EscClose the big code, or end the screensaver

On every phone

▲Raise your hand: names light up on your screen in the order hands went up. Click the hand counter to lower them.
●Notebook: every brief, rule, card and result a student collects, to keep.
■The Guide: a poster tour of every module and how the phone plays it.
◆Sit out: busy students can skip a round and jump back in.

7Vote, timer & more

the classroom tools in the top bar · on every screen

Top bar ▸ Vote

The room vote

Every phone becomes a ballot. The big screen counts live and crowns the winner, with a spotlight if it’s a tie. Click Vote and choose what to vote on:

The next moduleEvery module is on the ballot. Most votes wins, then you set it up.
Your own questionType a question and as many answers as you like. Press Enter for the next answer. Your last question is remembered, so you can run it again.

Use it for anything: which track to finish first, the next topic, a band name, a quick “did that make sense?”. Secret ballot hides the counts until you close the vote. Enter closes it.

Top bar ▸ Timer · in every game too

The room timer

Pick 1–30 minutes, or type any time, and press Start. The button counts down, and so does every phone.

While it runsPhones waiting between games show the countdown full screen; during a game it sits small at the top. Click the button to pause, add a minute or stop. The last 10 seconds turn red.
When it endsThe big screen flashes TIME! (silently). Every phone vibrates, gives one soft boop and shows Time’s up! until the student taps it away. (iPhones can’t vibrate from a website: they get the screen and the boop.)
Top bar ▸ Pick

Pick at random

One student: a spotlight hops over the names and lands on someone. A group of 2–12. Teams: 2–8 teams, dealt out evenly and named after a colour and a shape (“Red Circle”); copy them with one click.

With Fair turns, nobody is picked twice until everyone has had a turn. The picked phone says You’re up!; in Teams every phone shows its team. Space picks again.

Top bar ▸ the C/// logo

Screensaver

Need the projector to go quiet? Click the Catalyst logo: the screen turns black with the white logo in the middle. Click to switch to white. Esc brings Control Room back.

The room keeps running underneath: phones stay connected and nothing is lost.

Also on the main menu: ⧉ Copy attendance (no sheet) and your teacher link. Raised hands show in the top bar too.

9Help & privacy

quick answers

A phone can’t join
  • Check the phone has internet (school Wi-Fi sometimes blocks new devices: mobile data works too).
  • Press Q and scan again, or open gunnitynes.github.io/Control-Room on the phone and type the four-letter room code from the big screen.
  • “Already in the room on another phone”: the name is taken by a phone that is still connected. Remove the wrong one by clicking its name chip on the room panel.
On school Wi-Fi, phones or computers can’t connect
  • Many school networks stop devices in a room from talking to each other. Control Room and CADCAST then send the traffic through a relay on the internet (Cloudflare), on the same port as ordinary websites. This happens by itself: nothing to set up.
  • Audio that goes through the relay is sent at 128 kb/s instead of 320 kb/s; the student’s CADCAST page says “through the relay”.
  • Still stuck? Try one phone on mobile data. If even that fails, the school may be blocking the internet services themselves: ask IT to allow peerjs.com and cloudflare.com.
A computer doesn’t pair or shows the wrong class
  • The CADCAST page must know your name: use the link from your main menu (it carries ?t=Your+Name), or type your name in “Your teacher’s name”.
  • If the main menu warns that another Control Room is open with your name, close the other tab, or add your last name.
  • Control Room must be open on your computer while students pair.
I can’t hear a student’s computer
  • The Mixer tab must be open in the same Chrome as Control Room.
  • On their side: the DAW plays into the Multi-Output Device, the CADCAST page says Connected, and Chrome was allowed to use the microphone.
  • “Paired · no sound yet” in the Mixer means the page is paired but nothing is playing into BlackHole.
The sound is on the wrong speakers

Control Room plays through your computer’s current output. Set the room speakers in your system sound settings before the lesson.

Privacy
  • No accounts and no tracking. Students give a first name or tap one from your list.
  • Your class list goes from your sheet to your computer and the phones in the room, nowhere else. Check-ins go back to your sheet only.
  • Sound and files between computers travel directly over the room’s network; the free PeerJS relay only introduces the devices to each other.
  • Your music, stems and photos stay in your browser.

One room. One big screen. Everybody plays.
